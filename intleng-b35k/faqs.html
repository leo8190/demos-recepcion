  • A long block engine is a fully assembled engine that includes the engine block, crankshaft, pistons, cylinder head(s), camshaft(s), valves, and internal components.
    It does not include external parts such as the alternator, power steering pump, starter, intake/exhaust manifolds, or accessories. It’s a ready-to-install foundation for a complete engine install.

  • A rebuilt or reconditioned engine is your original engine that has been completely disassembled, cleaned, inspected, and reassembled with new or refurbished components such as bearings, seals, gaskets, and rings. This restores the engine to factory specifications or better, giving long-term reliability and performance.

  • External accessories are components mounted on the outside of the long block, including:

    • Alternator

    • Starter

    • A/C compressor

    • Power steering pump

    • Intake and exhaust manifolds

    • Timing Components

    • Valve & Timing Covers

    • Oil Pan

    • Sensors, wiring harness, and brackets
      These components are inspected, but they are not included with the long block or warranty unless otherwise noted.

  • During every engine rebuild or installation, we inspect your external engine accessories — these are the components that bolt onto the outside of the long block (such as the alternator, starter, power steering pump, A/C compressor, sensors, and belts).

    After teardown and inspection, we call you with your engine report.
    This report includes:

    • Whether your core was rebuildable

    • What external parts are bad, worn, or not functioning

    • What parts are recommended to replace

    • Pricing for each part

    • Your approval before anything is installed

    We always start with parts that are bad or failing, because these components can affect the performance and lifespan of your engine.

    If you approve the recommended parts, we install them during the rebuild or installation process and simply add the parts to your remaining balance — no hidden fees, no surprises.

    This ensures your engine runs correctly and prevents future breakdowns caused by worn external accessories.

  • A rebuildable core is your old engine that can be disassembled and restored. To qualify, it must have:

    • No cracked or damaged block

    • No cracked cylinder heads

    • No missing major parts

    • No severe internal damage that prevents machining
      If your core is not rebuildable, a core charge will apply to cover the cost of sourcing another engine for the rebuild.

  • If your old engine is seized beyond repair, missing major components, cracked, or damaged beyond machining, you will be charged an additional core fee. This fee varies depending on engine type and availability.
    We will inspect your core during teardown and notify you immediately if a charge applies.cription

  • Our rebuild process includes:

    • Complete teardown and internal inspection

    • Cleaning and machining of the block and heads

    • Replacement of piston rings, bearings, gaskets, seals.

    • Precision assembly and torquing to factory specifications

    • Final testing and quality checks.

      You can view the full Scope Sheet on our Services page.

  • Most rebuilds can take 7–10 business days, depending on parts availability, inspection results, and workload.
    We provide an estimated timeline after inspection.

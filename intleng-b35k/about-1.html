About

 

History

Established in 2015, International Engines LLC was built on more than 30 years of hands-on experience in the engine and transmission industry.
After years of selling and installing quality Japanese and domestic powertrains in California, we decided to open our own facility to better serve customers throughout Phoenix, Avondale, and the surrounding areas.

Over the years, we’ve sold and installed thousands of engines and transmissions from everyday drivers to professional repair shops and continue to grow by providing honest service and dependable results.

Specialties

From Honda to Ford, we specialize in all Japanese and Domestic engines and transmissions.
Whether you’re looking for a used, rebuilt, or reconditioned engine, our goal is the same: to ensure your complete satisfaction with every job.

If you prefer installation, we offer installation package deals that include:

  • Complete removal and installation

  • Full inspection before work begins

  • Transparent, upfront pricing

We believe in quality over quantity, and we stand behind every job we perform.

Our Warranty Promise

All of our work is backed by strong warranty coverage:

  • Engines: Up to 3 years / 36,000 miles

  • Transmissions: Up to 1 year / 12,000 miles

Warranty coverage applies to installations performed by our shop, ensuring you’re protected and confident in your repair.

Our Process

Before we begin any job, we inspect your vehicle at no charge to identify the real cause of failure.
We’ll explain what needs to be repaired, what’s optional, and provide a clear estimate no surprises.

If your vehicle is:

  • Overheating

  • Knocking

  • Burning oil or coolant

  • Slipping or not shifting

We’ll offer you the best repair or replacement options that fit your needs and budget.

Our Commitment

At International Engines LLC, we believe in honesty, quality, and value.
We sell top-quality Japanese and Domestic engines and transmissions, all covered by warranty and tested for performance.

Our team takes pride in giving every customer a fair price, professional service, and long-lasting results.

Transmission FAQs

Everything You Need to Know About Transmission Repairs

Clear answers. Honest guidance. No confusion,  just the information you need before making a repair decision.

How do I know if my transmission needs to be rebuilt?

Common signs include slipping gears, delayed shifting, grinding, fluid leaks, or warning lights. We offer free diagnostics to confirm the issue before recommending repairs.

How long does a transmission rebuild take?

Most rebuilds are completed within a few business days, depending on the vehicle and parts availability. We prioritize fast turnaround without sacrificing quality.

Do you offer a warranty on rebuilt transmissions?

Yes. All rebuilt units are backed by a 12/36 month warranty for your peace of mind.

Is rebuilding cheaper than replacing a transmission?

In many cases, yes. Rebuilding restores your existing unit at a lower cost than a full factory replacement while maintaining performance and reliability.

Do you work with other repair shops?

Yes. We provide wholesale transmission rebuild services for repair facilities across the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

Proper Transmission Maintenance Extends Vehicle Life

Routine fluid service and early diagnostics can prevent costly rebuilds and increase the lifespan of your transmission.

Maintenance questions, answered straight

Is dealer maintenance required to keep my warranty?

No — that is a myth. By law, any qualified shop can perform your scheduled maintenance without voiding the factory warranty. Keep your receipts and you are covered.

What does a diagnostic actually tell you?

The scanner gives us codes and live data; the skill is interpreting them. A code rarely says "replace this part" — it says "look here." We look, confirm the actual fault, and quote the real fix.

How much is a pre-purchase inspection worth?

Usually the best money you will spend on a used car. Finding a leaking transmission or hidden crash damage before you buy can save you thousands — or give you negotiating power.

FULLY CERTIFIED

Established in 1977, Reinaldo Paint & Body Shop is a family-owned business that specializes in auto bodywork repairs and detailing. We guarantee our customers’ satisfaction through quality work and friendly customer service.

Reinaldo Paint & Body Shop in Miami Florida is a fully certified Gold Class I-Car, ASE and Geico Express auto repair facility specializes in collision repair and refinish for all major insurance companies and all types of vehicles.
Reinaldo Paint & Body Shop counts on highly trained technicians that are certified to ensure your repair or project matches or exceeds industry standards. Count on our experience and resources to repair from minor dents and scratches to large structural damage.

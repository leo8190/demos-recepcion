About Poblano's — Irving's Family Auto Shop

Honest mechanics, fair prices, and bilingual service — right here in Irving, TX

Our Story

Poblano's Tire Mechanic & Muffler Shop was built on a simple belief: every customer deserves honest, quality auto repair at a fair price — regardless of whether they speak English or Spanish. Located at 1502 Rock Island Rd in Irving, Texas, we've served families, commuters, and businesses throughout the Irving and greater DFW area.

As a family-owned and operated shop, we understand how much you depend on your vehicle — for work, family, and everyday life. That's why we take every repair seriously, explain every recommendation clearly, and never charge for work that isn't needed. When you bring your car to Poblano's, you're treated like family.

Our bilingual team — fluent in both English and Spanish — proudly serves Irving's diverse community, including customers from Grand Prairie, Las Colinas, Euless, Bedford, Carrollton, and the greater Dallas-Fort Worth area. We are conveniently located near DFW Airport and Las Colinas.

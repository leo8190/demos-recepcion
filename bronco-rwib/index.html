What we do

General auto repair, done in-house

Both shops handle the work below on most cars, trucks, vans and SUVs.

  • Check-engine diagnostics

    Code pull, live data and a real cause — not a guess and a parts bill.

  • Brakes

    Pads, rotors, calipers, hoses, lines and brake fluid service.

  • A/C and heat

    Leak check, evacuate and recharge, compressors, condensers, blower motors.

  • Suspension and steering

    Shocks, struts, control arms, ball joints, tie rods, CV axles, wheel bearings.

  • Oil, fluids and filters

    Conventional and synthetic oil changes, coolant, brake and power steering fluid.

  • Batteries and electrical

    Starters, alternators, charging system tests, sensors, wiring and lighting.

  • Cooling system

    Radiators, water pumps, thermostats, hoses, fans and overheating diagnosis.

  • Engine repair

    Timing belts and chains, head gaskets, oil leaks, misfires, belts and pulleys.

  • Exhaust and emissions prep

    Mufflers, pipes, sensors, and getting you ready for Dallas County emissions.

Not performed at either shop

We don't do transmission work

No rebuilds, no replacements, no internal transmission repair — at West Dallas or Oak Cliff. We'd rather turn the job down than do it halfway.

If your symptoms point at the transmission, we'll still put it on the scanner, tell you straight what we're seeing, and send you to a transmission specialist we trust.

MEDINA'S AUTOMOTIVE

Since 1983, the Medina family has maintained the legacy put into place by their progenitor Hector Medina 4 generations ago. Hector Medina was one of the first ASE certified automotive service technicians in the state of Texas. His experience was developed by his extensive work at El Paso area Ford & Chrysler dealerships. Medina's Automotive is built on the philosophy of treating our customer's cars like we would our own prized vehicles and working hard to retain those customers for a lifetime.

For your convenience, we have two locations in Phoenix to provide the maximum volume of service. Our 7th Avenue location is run by Hector's son Ernesto. And both locations work together to bring you the talent that you need for any specific repair.

Comprehensive Auto Repair

We offer a full scope of automotive repair that covers both exterior and interior issues. If your instrument cluster is malfunctioning or your headlights are dimming, we can solve any problem. Whether it is electrical, mechanical, or computer-centric in nature, we have the full-service shop bays and tools to diagnose the problems.

You should also come to us for oil changes. We know exactly what grade and type of oil your motor needs to operate at its peak capacity. This will extend the life of your motor, your seals, and save you from having to rebuild or junk it a premature age. It is not unheard of for motors to reach 1 million miles if they run on a fully synthetic fluid.

If you need auto repair in an emergency, we will try our best to get you up and running. Call us when your battery dies, your tire goes flat, or you simply can't start your vehicle in the morning. We cater to the needs of the community and pull out all the stops to squeeze in emergency situations for your convenience.

Why You Should Never Go to the Dealerships

The dealership service department is focused chiefly on general maintenance of late-model vehicles. The types of mechanics that they hire work on flat rates and do not like to occupy their time with more complex work. 

There are very few true automotive repair shops left anymore. The big commercial chains are focused mainly on repairing the common wear items. They don't like to deal with complex problems either. 

They would rather change oil and brake pads all day because these are the chief repair issues that a brand-new vehicle requires. The dealership also marks up its parts by 800 percent or more. We can sell you the same computer for $200 that they are listing for $2000.

Because we are flexible and try to fit into your budget, people with used automobiles that are no longer under a full warranty like to come to us. We help them solve major repairs and those little things that occur only with age. 

Because the species of repairs that we carry out are more focused on used vehicles that have some wear, we can spot common repair items like worn belts, leaky gaskets, bad wheel bearings, tire tread wear alignment issues, and performance problems.

This makes us the best value for your money because we are dedicated to helping people just like you. The dealerships like to pad up the price of repairs so high on an older vehicle that you will feel compelled to just buy a new one. This keeps people stuck on a debt treadmill with continual monthly payments. 

We can keep your used vehicle running like-new forever. And this saves you money and gives you the flexibility to retain a backup vehicle for commuting and long-distance road trips even if you do decide to buy a new one. 

Having an extra car that runs great is also a gem on the used car market. When you keep all the service records and can show that a reputable shop like Medina's kept up all the repairs, it is easy to demand top dollar.

SERVICES

BRAKE REPAIR

When you need brake repair services, you can count on Medina's to provide you with numerous options for premium pads and rotors. We install everything from ceramic to semi-metallic pads and can even obtain performance rotors. 

You can also count on us providing other services such as brake flushes that improve braking and pedal-feel considerably. Whether you have a hydraulic fluid leak or just a mushy pedal, it is important to take care of the problems as soon as possible. 

One of the biggest risks of getting into an accident is brakes that perform poorly. All it takes is some distractions during stop-and-go driving or some bad weather to smack right into another vehicle.

ENGINE REPAIR

There are numerous sensors, belts, vacuum hoses, and gears that make your motor run. If any of these pieces are malfunctioning, you can count on your engine performing badly. A timing belt, for example, can slip just a half a tooth and lead to heavy chugging and a huge loss of power. 

If the crankshaft sensor is damaged, the vehicle won't even start. Yet, this error can be tricky because it usually doesn't throw any perceivable code to pick it up with a diagnostic scan tool. In addition, you can have oil pressure problems, coking from burnt oil, sludge, and numerous other problems that can melt your engine overnight. 

TRANSMISSION REPAIR

We specialize in transmission repair services. We stock a wide variety of remanufactured transmissions that save you considerable costs off the price and come with warranties just like the factory models. We first check the computer codes to pinpoint any electrical problems or fluid pressure issues. 

We can then connect gauges and check the quality of the fluid to see if it is burnt as an indication of internal damage. We can also pull the pan and look for metal shavings that would indicate severe malfunctions that require a full rebuild. The hydraulic transmission is the most complex part in an automatic vehicle and is one of the hardest jobs to replace and repair.

We also fix manual transmissions by adjusting the linkage, replacing worn shifter components, flushing the gear oil, and by replacing clutches, seals, and pressure plates. We can fully rebuild manual transmissions or replace them with remanufactured units that are backed by a warranty. 

AIR CONDITIONING REPAIR

Vehicles in Phoenix can generate lots of heat. That’s why our customers often turn to repair and upgrade the air conditioning systems in their cars and trucks. Our staff can quickly and efficiently repair any type of issues with the AC and install the latest and most effective air conditioning systems to keep the interior of the vehicles cool when there’s blazing heat outside and nice and toasty for vehicles in freezing weather. 

We are air conditioning system experts and know how to keep the interior of your vehicle at the perfect temperature With a free A/C inspection we adjust and tighten hose connections, test for leak, and get your system cooling you down in no time. Our shop are also equipped with 1234yf refrigerant and the machinery required to service newer vehicles.

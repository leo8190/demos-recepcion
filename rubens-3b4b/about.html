Ruben's Auto Repair & A/C Service is a local Houston shop serving drivers in the Aldine area and beyond. With over 30 years in the business, we focus on what matters most: accurate diagnostics, clear communication, and repairs you can trust—especially when Texas heat makes a working A/C non-negotiable.

We work on everyday vehicles and specialize in keeping older vehicles (2015 & older / 7–10+ years) reliable with practical repairs and scheduled maintenance. We diagnose first—so you're not guessing.

From A/C problems and overheating issues to brake concerns and Check Engine Lights, we take the time to pinpoint the cause and explain your options before any work begins.

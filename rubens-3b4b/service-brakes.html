Don't Ignore the Signs

Is It Time for Brake Service?

Your brakes give clear warnings before they fail. Recognize these signs early and stay safe.

Squealing or Squeaking

A high-pitched squeal when braking is the built-in wear indicator telling you the pads are thin and need replacement soon.

Grinding Metal Sound

Metal-on-metal grinding means the pads are completely worn and the caliper is cutting into the rotor. This causes expensive damage fast.

Soft or Spongy Pedal

If the brake pedal sinks to the floor or feels mushy, there may be air in the brake lines, a fluid leak, or a failing master cylinder.

Vibration When Braking

Pulsating or shaking through the pedal or steering wheel usually indicates warped rotors that need resurfacing or replacement.

Vehicle Pulls to One Side

If the car drifts left or right when braking, a caliper may be sticking or pads may be wearing unevenly on one side.

Brake Warning Light On

A brake warning light on the dashboard can indicate low fluid, worn pads, or an ABS system fault. Don’t ignore it.

Brake Services We Offer

From a routine pad swap to full ABS diagnostics — we handle every component of your braking system.

01

Complete Brake Inspection

We measure pad thickness, check rotor condition, inspect calipers, hoses, and lines, and test fluid quality. The full picture before any repair.

02

Brake Pad Replacement

Quality ceramic or semi-metallic pads matched to your vehicle and driving conditions. Includes hardware and lubrication of slide pins.

03

Rotor Resurfacing & Replacement

We measure rotor thickness and runout. If they can be resurfaced, we machine them. If not, we replace them with quality rotors.

04

Caliper Repair & Replacement

Sticking or seized calipers cause uneven wear and pulling. We rebuild or replace calipers and ensure even braking force.

05

Brake Hose & Line Replacement

Cracked rubber hoses and corroded steel lines are leak risks. We inspect and replace them to maintain full hydraulic pressure.

06

Brake Fluid Flush & Bleed

Brake fluid absorbs moisture over time, reducing boiling point and braking performance. We flush and bleed the system with fresh DOT-spec fluid.

07

ABS Module & Sensor Diagnostics

ABS light on? We scan the module, test wheel speed sensors, and diagnose electronic braking faults with professional-grade tools.

08

Brake Warning Light Diagnosis

We trace the cause of your brake warning light — whether it’s low fluid, pad wear sensors, or an ABS fault code.

09

Emergency / Parking Brake Repair

Parking brake not holding or dragging? We adjust, repair, or replace cables, shoes, and mechanisms.

10

Drum Brake Service

Rear drum brake inspection, shoe replacement, hardware kits, and wheel cylinder repair for vehicles with drum brake systems.

Don't Ignore the Signs

Is Your A/C Failing?

Don't wait until it's 100°F outside. If you notice any of these, bring your vehicle in.

Warm Air from Vents

Your A/C is blowing but the air isn’t cold. Often points to low refrigerant, a failing compressor, or a clogged expansion valve.

Strange Noises When A/C Runs

Clicking, rattling, or grinding when the A/C kicks on can indicate a worn compressor clutch or debris in the blower motor.

Musty or Sour Smell

A moldy smell from the vents usually means bacteria or mold growth in the evaporator core or a dirty cabin air filter.

Water on Passenger Floor

Puddles or dampness on the passenger-side floorboard can signal a clogged condensate drain line.

Compressor Clutch Won't Engage

If you don't hear the compressor click on, the system may be critically low on refrigerant or have an electrical fault.

Weak Airflow from Vents

Air barely comes out even on the highest setting. Could be a failing blower motor, clogged cabin filter, or duct issue.

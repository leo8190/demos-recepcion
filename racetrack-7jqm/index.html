FAQ

You ask, we answer

If you’re unsure about anything, this is the place to get clarity.

How long will my repair take?

Most routine services are completed within 1-2 hours. More complex repairs vary—we’ll give you an accurate estimate after diagnosis.

Can I wait while my car is being serviced?

Yes! We have a comfortable waiting area with free coffee and Wi-Fi. For longer jobs, we’ll keep you updated by phone or text.

What kind of vehicles do you work on?

We service all makes and models—domestic and foreign. Our ASE-certified technicians have experience with virtually every vehicle type.

Do you use OEM parts?

We offer both OEM and quality aftermarket parts. We’ll discuss options and help you choose the best fit for your budget.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes! We stand behind our work. If you have any concerns after service, bring your vehicle back and we’ll make it right.

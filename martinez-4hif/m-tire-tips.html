TIRE TIPS

Taking Care of Your Tires
  1. Every month, be sure to check and adjust your tire pressure to its proper psi.
  2. Before driving your car, take a minute to walk around your vehicle and inspect the tires for damage or abnormal wear.
  3. Don’t ever overinflate your tires.
  4. When the tread has worn down, replace your tires and be sure to replace in matched pairs or a complete set.
  5. Rely on the professionals at Martinez Tire & Auto Center to provide you with expert advice on the selection, care and maintenance of tires for your vehicle.

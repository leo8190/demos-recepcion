AC & Cooling System

Texas heat is brutal. We keep your cab cold and your engine cool — on the same visit.

Get a Free Estimate

What It Is

AC and engine cooling system repair — cab climate control plus radiators, water pumps, fan clutches and EGR coolers. We keep both you and your engine from overheating.

What's Included

  • AC recharge & leak repair
  • Compressor & condenser replacement
  • Radiator service & replacement
  • Water pump replacement
  • Fan clutch service
  • EGR & charge air cooler
  • Coolant flush

Signs You Need This Service

  • Cab AC blowing warm or weak air
  • Engine temp climbing on grades
  • Coolant on the ground or low reservoir
  • Steam or sweet smell from the engine
  • Check engine light from EGR cooler

ABOUT

At Roger’s Motors, we provide reliable automotive maintenance and repair services with a strong focus on honesty, quality workmanship, and clear communication.

We operate inside XPRESS Auto Repair, located in Phoenix, Arizona, allowing us to offer professional service in a fully equipped workshop while maintaining a personalized approach with every customer.

With over 15 years of hands-on experience, we specialize in preventive maintenance, oil changes, brake services, engine diagnostics, and general mechanical repairs. Our goal is to keep your vehicle safe, reliable, and performing at its best.

We proudly serve the Phoenix area and welcome both English and Spanish-speaking customers. We speak Spanish and are always happy to explain the work being done so you can feel confident in every service.

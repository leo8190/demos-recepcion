OUR TEAM

Experienced European Vehicle Service Specialists

As your trusted fine European motors auto services specialist, we vow to provide exceptional services all the way around the board. When you schedule an appointment with us, expect industry experts who treat your automobile like it's their very own. We understand how important your vehicle is and take every possible step to keep it protected while at our shop while providing the absolute best possible repairs, regardless of the type of woes you’re experiencing with the vehicle.

With more than 38-years working as a certified Mercedes Benz specialists, our technicians bring the expertise that you can count on to repair your car. We service all European model vehicles but specialize in the Mercedes Benz model vehicles. No matter the vehicle model or year, our technicians have the expertise to efficiently handle your service needs so you’re back on the road fast.

Best Auto Body Service in Town

Our state of the art auto body shop has all of the latest and greatest tools and equipment needed to service your European engine vehicle. Our well-trained technicians take the time to do things right the first time around, minimizing return visits and hassles with vehicle breakdowns. We’ll start with an inspection to ensure a proper diagnosis of the trouble. Once the culprit of trouble is determined, we’ll then quickly go to work to service the vehicle so you’re not off the road any longer than necessary.

While we understand that you want your vehicle back as fast as possible and we strive to make this happen, cutting corners to rush through the job just isn’t our thing. Rest assured we take the time to do things right when we service your car. Your vehicle is a reflection of who you are and we make sure that it maintains its beauty, integrity, and that beautiful purr of the motor when you drive down the street.

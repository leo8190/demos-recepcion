ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

Your Premier Destination for Diesel Excellence

We are a fully equipped shop specializing in the in the repair and maintenance of light and medium duty diesel powered trucks. We handle everything from oil changes and routine maintenance to full engine rebuilds. Today's modern diesels need the right technical knowledge and equipment for proper diagnostic and repairs. Don't let some one guess or play games with your hard earned money and your valuable time. We will fix your vehicle right the first time period.

Customer satisfaction is number one, and we will do everything in our power to make you a loyal and satisfied customer. 

 

We offer quality work and friendly service at fair price. We believe in making customers for life not one time shoppers. 


We work with most extended warranties, and service fleet vehicles. Our Goal is to get you back on the road with fast, quality, and affordable service.

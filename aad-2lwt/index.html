WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Diesel Experts

We are a fully certified diesel repair shop specializing in the repair and maintenance of light and medium duty diesel powered trucks. Let our trained diesel mechanics attend to your diesel engine repair or service needs. Discover the difference at All About Diesel, your go-to destination for diesel repair and maintenance in Miami and surrounding areas. As ASE certified professionals, we take pride in our expertise, ensuring top-notch service for Cummins, Ford Powerstroke, and Chevy Duramax engines.


We're not just a repair shop – we're a family-owned business committed to your satisfaction. Fluent in Spanish, we provide personalized service to meet your needs. Plus, we work with most warranties and service fleet vehicles, offering quality work at affordable prices. Trust us to keep your diesel vehicle running smoothly and reliably, mile after mile.
